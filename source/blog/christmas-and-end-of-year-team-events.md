The classic office Christmas party is losing its shine, but the need to close the year together has not gone away. If you are looking for end-of-year team event ideas that people will remember for the right reasons, this guide covers twelve formats, from a festive weekend in a European capital to a January kickoff in the sun, plus the planning details that make the difference.

## Why the traditional Christmas party needs a rethink

A survey of 1,203 UK employees carried out by Censuswide for NerdWallet UK in November 2023 found that fewer than one in ten (9%) wanted a Christmas party. Nearly two in five (39%) said they would prefer a voucher or gift card, and 29% would rather have an extra day off. Yet 69% said they would still attend if their employer held one.

The lesson is not to cancel the celebration. It is to design one with a real purpose: time together that people would not get otherwise, a shared experience rather than another evening in a function room, and a format that works for everyone, including colleagues who do not drink or who have family commitments in the evening.

## Festive city trips

### 1. A Christmas market weekend in Central Europe
Vienna, Prague, Budapest and Kraków are at their most atmospheric in December. A two-night trip can combine a morning workshop to review the year, a guided walk through the old town, the markets in the late afternoon and a team dinner. See our guides to [Vienna](#retraite-vienne), [Prague](#retraite-prague), [Budapest](#retraite-budapest) and [Kraków](#retraite-cracovie).

### 2. A festive food tour
Food brings people together without forcing anyone to perform. A guided tasting walk through a market hall or old quarter works in almost any city and suits mixed groups. Lisbon and Seville are both excellent in December. [Discover Lisbon](#retraite-lisbonne) · [Discover Seville](#retraite-seville).

### 3. A concert or cultural evening
Advent concerts, a private museum visit or a classical performance give the evening a sense of occasion. Vienna is the obvious choice, but many cities have seasonal programmes worth building a night around.

## Winter sun and nature

### 4. A year-end retreat in the sun
For distributed teams, an end-of-year retreat somewhere mild can replace a dozen local parties. The [Algarve](#retraite-algarve), [Madeira](#retraite-madere) and [Marrakech](#retraite-marrakech) all offer bright winter days and quieter venues. Our [winter offsite destinations](#post-winter-offsite-destinations-europe) article has more options.

### 5. A guided coastal or mountain walk
A half-day hike gives conversations room to happen naturally. Madeira's levada trails and the cliffs of the Algarve are classics; [Zakopane](#retraite-zakopane) offers snowy landscapes and wooden lodges for a more wintry feel.

### 6. A snow day
Snowshoeing, sledging or a beginner ski lesson are great levellers: nobody is an expert, and everybody laughs. Pair it with a long lunch in a mountain hut.

## Activity-based celebrations

### 7. A cooking class and shared dinner
Teams cook a festive menu together, then sit down to eat it. It is collaborative, inclusive and easy to adapt to dietary needs. Browse more ideas in our [activities](#activites).

### 8. A creative workshop
Ceramics, wreath making, chocolate making or a photography walk give people something to take home and a different way to interact with colleagues.

### 9. A charity or volunteering half-day
Many teams now dedicate part of their year-end gathering to a local cause, such as a food bank, a beach clean-up or a nature restoration project. It adds meaning without adding cost to the fun part.

### 10. A team challenge
A treasure hunt through an old town, an escape game or a sailing regatta brings out friendly competition. Our list of [team building activities adults enjoy](#post-team-building-activities-adults-enjoy) and the [team building format](#event-team-building) give you more inspiration.

## Moving the celebration to January

### 11. A New Year kickoff instead of a Christmas party
December diaries are crowded, venues are expensive and people are tired. A January or February gathering can celebrate last year and launch the next one at the same time, with awards, a plenary and an outdoor activity. If the year went well, reward the people who made it happen with a short trip in the new year. An [incentive trip](#event-incentive) can be the highlight of next year's calendar rather than one more December evening. See our [incentive trip ideas](#post-incentive-trip-ideas).

## Planning tips for end-of-year team events

**Start earlier than you think.** Tagvenue's analysis of its own enquiry data, published in November 2026, found that the typical Christmas party is enquired about 87 days before the event. If you are reading this in late November, a January date will give you far more choice than mid-December.

**Consider a weekday.** The same Tagvenue analysis found that Thursday receives 79% more Christmas party enquiries than Friday. Early-week dates or a daytime format are usually easier to secure.

**Make it inclusive.** Offer daytime and evening moments, plan alcohol-free options that feel just as special, and check dietary needs, accessibility and family constraints in advance.

**Set a clear purpose.** Is it a thank-you, a review of the year, a launch for next year or simply a celebration? A short brief helps everyone pull in the same direction. You can [send us your brief](#brief) to get started.

**Keep travel and logistics simple.** If people are flying in from several cities, centralise the bookings. We [book every participant's flight](#comment/vols) and arrange transfers so nobody is left to sort it out alone.

## FAQ

### What can we do instead of a Christmas party?
Popular alternatives include a Christmas market city trip, a cooking class, a volunteering half-day, an outdoor activity day or a short retreat in the sun. Many companies also move the celebration to January and combine it with a kickoff.

### When should we book an end-of-year team event?
As early as possible. Tagvenue's enquiry data shows companies typically start looking around three months before a Christmas party. For a trip with flights, earlier is better, and January dates often offer more availability.

### How do we make an end-of-year event inclusive?
Mix daytime and evening moments, offer attractive alcohol-free options, check dietary and accessibility needs in advance, and choose activities where no one needs special skills to take part.
