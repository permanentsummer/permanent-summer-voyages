Poland is one of Europe's best-kept secrets for company retreats. Beautiful historic cities, mountains, a Baltic coastline, excellent food and prices that leave room in the budget for memorable experiences. We are expanding our Poland programme and visiting partner hotels in person.

## Kraków: history and great value

Kraków's old town is one of the most beautiful in Europe, with a huge market square, the Wawel castle and the lively Kazimierz district. Modern hotels with meeting rooms sit a short walk from the centre.

- **Best for:** groups of any size, first retreats, value-focused teams.
- **Ideas:** a guided old town treasure hunt, a pierogi cooking class, a visit to the Wieliczka salt mine, a day trip to the mountains.

[Discover Kraków for retreats](#retraite-cracovie).

## Zakopane and the Tatra mountains

Two hours from Kraków, Zakopane sits at the foot of the Tatras. Wooden lodges, mountain hotels with spas, hiking trails in summer and snow in winter.

- **Best for:** leadership retreats, nature-focused teams, winter offsites.
- **Ideas:** guided hikes, sleigh rides, a highlander dinner with live music, thermal pools.

[Discover Zakopane](#retraite-zakopane).

## Gdańsk and the Baltic coast

A colourful port city with a rich history, a lively waterfront and beaches nearby in Sopot. Great from May to September.

- **Best for:** summer retreats, sailing, culture.
- **Ideas:** a boat trip on the Motława river, a sailing regatta in the bay, a Baltic seafood dinner.

[Discover Gdańsk](#retraite-gdansk).

![Colourful historic buildings along the Gdańsk waterfront](bl11c)

## A sample 4-day programme

**Day 1:** fly into Kraków, opening session, dinner in Kazimierz.
**Day 2:** morning workshops, afternoon old town treasure hunt, cooking class.
**Day 3:** transfer to Zakopane, mountain hike, highlander dinner.
**Day 4:** closing session in the lodge, transfer back to Kraków airport.

## When to go

- **Kraków:** all year; May to September is warmest, December has Christmas markets.
- **Zakopane:** June to September for hiking, December to March for snow.
- **Gdańsk:** May to September.

## FAQ

### Is Poland good for a company retreat?
Very. It combines beautiful cities, mountains and coast with excellent food and some of the best prices in Europe.

### Where should we go in Poland for a team offsite?
Kraków for culture and value, Zakopane for mountains and winter, and Gdańsk for summer by the sea.

### Is Kraków easy to reach?
Yes. Kraków airport has direct flights from many European cities, and Zakopane is about two hours away by road.
