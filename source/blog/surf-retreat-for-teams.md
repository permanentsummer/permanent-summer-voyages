A surf retreat puts everyone on the same level: the CEO and the new hire fall off the board in exactly the same way. That shared experience, plus the energy of being in the ocean, is why surf offsites have become so popular with startups and remote teams.

## Why surf works for team building

- **Everyone starts together.** Most people are beginners, so nobody dominates.
- **It's naturally supportive.** People cheer each other on for every wave caught.
- **It clears the head.** A morning in the water makes afternoon workshops sharper.
- **It's memorable.** Teams talk about it for years.

## The best surf destinations for teams

### Ericeira, Portugal
A World Surfing Reserve 45 minutes from Lisbon airport, with beginner-friendly beaches, surf schools used to groups, and hotels with meeting rooms by the ocean. Our favourite in Europe. [Discover Ericeira](#retraite-ericeira).

### The Algarve, Portugal
The west coast near Sagres offers consistent waves and a wild landscape, with the comfort of the south coast nearby. [Discover the Algarve](#retraite-algarve).

### Taghazout, Morocco
Sun almost all year and mellow point breaks, ideal for winter retreats. [Discover Taghazout](#retraite-taghazout).

## How to structure a surf offsite

The classic rhythm:

- **Morning:** two-hour surf lesson with certified instructors (wetsuits and boards provided).
- **Lunch:** together, close to the beach.
- **Afternoon:** workshops or strategy sessions.
- **Evening:** dinner in the harbour or a team activity.

For a three-day retreat, two surf sessions are enough. Offer an alternative such as yoga, an e-bike ride or a coastal hike for anyone who prefers to stay dry.

## What beginners should know

- No experience is needed. Instructors start on the sand.
- Wetsuits are worn all year on the Atlantic coast.
- Participants should be comfortable in water.
- Groups are split by level, with small instructor ratios.

## Best season

April to October is ideal for beginners in Portugal. Taghazout works all year, with the gentlest conditions in autumn and winter.

## FAQ

### Do participants need surfing experience?
No. Lessons are designed for complete beginners, with instructors and all equipment provided.

### What is the best place in Europe for a surf retreat?
Ericeira in Portugal, thanks to beginner-friendly beaches, quality schools and its location close to Lisbon airport.

### Can we work during a surf retreat?
Yes. The usual format is surf in the morning and workshops in the afternoon, when energy and focus are high.
