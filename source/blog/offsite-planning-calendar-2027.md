Timing changes everything: weather, prices, venue availability and flight costs. Here is our month-by-month guide to planning your 2027 retreat in Europe, with when to book each window.

## January and February

**Best for:** sales kickoffs, winter sun, snow.
**Where:** [Madeira](#retraite-madere), [Seville](#retraite-seville), the [Algarve](#retraite-algarve), [Taghazout](#retraite-taghazout), or the mountains in [Zakopane](#retraite-zakopane).
**Book by:** September or October of the previous year.
**Watch out for:** February school holidays in ski resorts.

## March and April

**Best for:** great-value retreats as spring arrives.
**Where:** [Lisbon](#retraite-lisbonne), [Marrakech](#retraite-marrakech), [Crete](#retraite-crete) from late April.
**Book by:** December or January.
**Watch out for:** Easter, which falls on **28 March 2027**. Expect busy flights and venues around it.

## May and June

**Best for:** the classic retreat window, with warm weather and long days.
**Where:** almost anywhere in southern Europe: [Mallorca](#retraite-majorque), [Ericeira](#retraite-ericeira), [Athens](#retraite-athenes), [Tuscany](#retraite-toscane), the [Peloponnese](#retraite-peloponnese).
**Book by:** January to February. These months fill first.
**Watch out for:** public holidays such as Ascension and Whit Monday in several countries.

## July and August

**Best for:** northern and mountain destinations, or flexible teams.
**Where:** [Gdańsk](#retraite-gdansk), [Zakopane](#retraite-zakopane), the [Azores](#retraite-acores).
**Book by:** February to March.
**Watch out for:** peak prices and heat in southern Europe; many people are on holiday.

## September and October

**Best for:** the second classic window, with warm seas and lower prices than summer. September is one of the most booked months for offsites.
**Where:** the [Algarve](#retraite-algarve), [Crete](#retraite-crete), [Porto and the Douro](#retraite-porto) for the harvest, [Kraków](#retraite-cracovie).
**Book by:** March to April.

## November and December

**Best for:** end-of-year retreats, client events and festive cities.
**Where:** [Vienna](#retraite-vienne), [Prague](#retraite-prague), [Budapest](#retraite-budapest) and [Kraków](#retraite-cracovie) for Christmas markets; [Marrakech](#retraite-marrakech) or [Madeira](#retraite-madere) for sun.
**Book by:** June to July.

## General booking rules

- **Under 50 people:** book three to six months ahead.
- **50 to 150 people:** six to nine months ahead.
- **150+ people or peak dates:** nine to twelve months ahead.
- **Flights:** book eight to twelve weeks before at the latest. Prices rise sharply after that. See our [group flights guide](#post-group-flights-for-company-retreats).

## FAQ

### What is the best month for a company retreat in Europe?
May, June, September and early October offer the best balance of weather, prices and availability in southern Europe.

### When should we book a company retreat for 2027?
Three to six months ahead for small groups, and six to nine months ahead for larger groups or peak months.

### When is Easter 2027?
Easter Sunday falls on 28 March 2027. Flights and venues are busier around that weekend in many European countries.
