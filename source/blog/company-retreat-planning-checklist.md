Use this checklist to keep your retreat on track. It assumes about 12 weeks of lead time, which is the minimum we recommend for a group of 20 to 60 people. For larger groups, start earlier.

## 12 weeks before

- [ ] Write the goal of the retreat in one sentence.
- [ ] Agree the budget per person, **flights included**. Use the [budget calculator](#budget).
- [ ] Fix the dates and check public holidays in every country your team lives in.
- [ ] List participants and their departure cities.
- [ ] Shortlist two or three [destinations](#retraites) that are easy to reach for everyone.

## 10 weeks before

- [ ] Request proposals from venues (or [ask us for proposals](#appel)).
- [ ] Visit or video-tour the shortlisted venues.
- [ ] Check meeting rooms: natural light, breakout spaces, Wi-Fi, screens.
- [ ] Confirm the venue and sign the contract. Read the cancellation terms.

## 8 weeks before

- [ ] Book flights for the whole group. Prices rise sharply after this point. See our guide to [group flights](#post-group-flights-for-company-retreats).
- [ ] Plan transfers between airport and venue.
- [ ] Collect dietary requirements, accessibility needs and room preferences.
- [ ] Choose activities. Browse [team-building activities](#activites).

![A team brainstorming with sticky notes](bl04b)

## 6 weeks before

- [ ] Draft the programme: one third work, one third shared experiences, one third free time.
- [ ] Brief any facilitators or speakers.
- [ ] Book restaurants for dinners outside the venue.
- [ ] Arrange travel insurance if needed.

## 4 weeks before

- [ ] Send a "save the details" email: dates, flights, venue, what to expect.
- [ ] Ask participants for passport details if needed (outside the Schengen area, such as [Marrakech](#retraite-marrakech) or [Istanbul](#retraite-istanbul)).
- [ ] Prepare workshop materials and pre-reads.
- [ ] Confirm final numbers with the venue.

## 2 weeks before

- [ ] Send the practical guide: flight times, transfers, weather, what to pack, the programme and one emergency contact.
- [ ] Share the room list with the venue.
- [ ] Confirm every supplier in writing.

## The week before

- [ ] Check flight times again and warn anyone affected by changes.
- [ ] Prepare name badges, printed agendas or a shared online doc.
- [ ] Create a group chat for real-time updates.

## During the retreat

- [ ] Keep one person reachable for every question, 24/7.
- [ ] Track arrivals and departures.
- [ ] Capture decisions and owners at the end of each session.
- [ ] Take photos for the internal recap.

## After the retreat

- [ ] Send a short survey within three days.
- [ ] Share decisions, owners and photos within a week.
- [ ] Reconcile the budget against the plan.
- [ ] Note what to repeat and what to change next time.

## FAQ

### How early should I start planning a company retreat?
Twelve weeks is the minimum for 20 to 60 people. For larger groups or peak dates, start six to nine months ahead.

### What is the most forgotten item on a retreat checklist?
Flights. Teams often book the venue first and the flights last, when prices are highest and arrival times no longer match.

### Who should own the retreat planning internally?
One person with decision power and time. Planning by committee slows every decision. If nobody has the time, an agency can take it over.
