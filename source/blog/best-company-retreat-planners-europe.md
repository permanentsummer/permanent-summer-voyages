Planning a retreat yourself takes time. That's why a whole industry of retreat planners has grown up in Europe. They differ a lot in how they work, what they include and who they suit. Here is an honest overview, written by a retreat agency, so we'll be clear about where we fit too.

## The main types of retreat planners

### Venue marketplaces with planners
Online platforms with a large catalogue of venues worldwide, plus planners who build a proposal. Strong on choice and a polished online experience. **TeamOut** is a well-known example, used by many international companies.

### Specialist retreat organisers
Companies focused on company retreats, often with curated venues and in-house programme design. **Surf Office**, one of the longest-established retreat organisers in Europe, and **Lá Fora**, which focuses on exclusive retreat venues, are examples.

### Seminar platforms and agencies (France)
Companies such as **Naboo** and **Kactus** help French teams find seminar venues, with platforms and agency services.

### Venue groups
Groups such as **Châteauform** operate their own seminar venues, so the venue and the service come together.

### Destination management companies (DMCs)
Local experts in one destination who handle logistics, activities and suppliers on the ground. Often used by agencies behind the scenes.

### Traditional travel agencies
Strong on flights and corporate travel, less specialised in retreat programmes.

## Questions to ask any retreat planner

1. **Do you book every participant's flight, or only the venue and programme?** For distributed teams this is the big one. Many planners leave flights to you.
2. **Who is my contact, and can I call them?** Before, during and after the trip.
3. **What exactly is included in the price per person?**
4. **How do you handle changes and cancellations?**
5. **Do you know the venues personally?**
6. **What is your fee model?** Commission, fixed fee or margin.
7. **Can you show examples of retreats like ours?**

## How to choose

- **Small team, one city, simple needs:** a venue marketplace or a venue group can be enough.
- **Distributed team, flights from many cities:** choose a planner that books flights too.
- **High-stakes leadership retreat:** look for personal service and venue knowledge.
- **Large kickoff or conference:** check event production experience.

## FAQ

### What does a company retreat planner do?
A retreat planner finds the venue, designs the programme and activities, manages suppliers and logistics, and supports the group during the trip. Some, like us, also book every participant's flight.

### How much do retreat planners charge?
Models vary: some earn commissions from venues, others charge a fixed fee or a margin on the total. Always ask what is included in the price per person.

### Is it worth using an agency for a company retreat?
For groups over 15 people, especially distributed teams, an agency usually saves dozens of hours and often money through supplier relationships.
