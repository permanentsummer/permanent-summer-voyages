Europe is the easiest continent in the world for a company retreat: short flights, huge variety and venues for every budget. The best destination for you depends on three things: where your team flies from, what the retreat must achieve, and the season.

Here are our favourites, grouped by what they do best.

## Sun, sea and easy flights

### The Algarve, Portugal
Golden cliffs, boutique hotels and quintas, golf, boat trips and more than 300 days of sun a year. Faro has direct flights from most European cities. Ideal from March to November. [Discover the Algarve](#retraite-algarve).

### Mallorca, Spain
Mountain villages, stone fincas and coves. Perfect for groups who want a mix of nature, culture and good food. Palma is one of Europe's best-connected airports. [Discover Mallorca](#retraite-majorque) or read our [Mallorca retreat guide](#post-mallorca-company-retreat-guide).

### Crete, Greece
Mountains, gorges and long sunny seasons, with authentic villages and an incredible food culture. [Discover Crete](#retraite-crete).

## City energy with culture

### Lisbon, Portugal
Light, tiles, rooftops and great food, with the coast 30 minutes away. One of the easiest cities in Europe to reach. [Discover Lisbon](#retraite-lisbonne).

### Barcelona, Spain
Architecture, beach and a buzzing food scene. Great for larger groups and [sales kickoffs](#event-kickoff). [Discover Barcelona](#retraite-barcelone).

### Athens, Greece
History at every corner and the Athens Riviera half an hour from the centre. [Discover Athens](#retraite-athenes).

## Surf and startup vibes

### Ericeira, Portugal
A World Surfing Reserve 45 minutes from Lisbon airport, with surf-friendly hotels, workshops by the ocean and fresh fish in the harbour. [Discover Ericeira](#retraite-ericeira) or read our [surf retreat guide](#post-surf-retreat-for-teams).

### Taghazout, Morocco
Year-round sun, surf and a relaxed atmosphere, a short flight from many European cities. [Discover Taghazout](#retraite-taghazout).

## Nature and calm

### Madeira and the Azores, Portugal
Dramatic landscapes, levada walks, whale watching and mild weather all year. [Madeira](#retraite-madere) · [the Azores](#retraite-acores).

### Zakopane, Poland
Mountain lodges in the Tatras, hiking in summer and snow in winter. [Discover Zakopane](#retraite-zakopane).

### Tuscany, Italy
Rolling hills, villas and long lunches. Perfect for [leadership retreats](#event-direction). [Discover Tuscany](#retraite-toscane).

## Great value

### Kraków, Poland
A beautiful old town, excellent food and some of the best value in Europe. [Discover Kraków](#retraite-cracovie) or read our [Poland retreat guide](#post-poland-company-retreat-guide).

### Porto and the Douro, Portugal
A characterful city and a wine valley an hour away. [Discover Porto](#retraite-porto).

### Budapest and Prague
Grand architecture, thermal baths and great prices. [Budapest](#retraite-budapest) · [Prague](#retraite-prague).

## Something different

### Marrakech and the Agafay desert, Morocco
Riads, desert camps under the stars and a short flight from Europe. [Discover Marrakech](#retraite-marrakech) or read our [Morocco offsite guide](#post-morocco-company-retreat-guide).

## How to choose

- **Map your airports first.** Direct flights for everyone beat a cheaper venue with connections.
- **Match the season.** Southern Europe is best in spring and autumn; cities work all year.
- **Match the goal.** Strategy needs calm; celebration needs energy.

## FAQ

### What is the best country in Europe for a company retreat?
Portugal is hard to beat: short flights from most of Europe, sun for most of the year, excellent venues and great value. Spain, Greece and Italy are close behind.

### What is the cheapest destination in Europe for a team offsite?
Poland, Hungary, the Czech Republic and northern Portugal offer some of the best value, especially Kraków, Budapest, Prague and Porto.

### Where can we do a company retreat in winter in Europe?
The Canary Islands, Madeira, the Algarve and Morocco stay mild in winter. For snow, look at the Alps or Zakopane. See our [winter offsite guide](#post-winter-offsite-destinations-europe).
