The format

A trip for your clients, distributors or partners: fewer meetings, more shared moments. We handle invitations, each guest's flights from their own city, a venue that makes an impression and a programme that leaves room to talk business without it feeling like work.

Who is it for?

  • Key accounts and loyal clients
  • Distributors and resellers
  • Partners and referrers
  • Launches with invited guests

Sample agenda

  1. Day 1: personal welcome, opening dinner
  2. Day 2: short presentation in the morning, local experience in the afternoon
  3. Day 3: free time and one-to-one meetings, gala dinner
  4. Day 4: departures

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle invitations?

Yes: registration form, flight details collection and RSVP tracking, in your branding.

Guests come from different countries?

That's the most common case. We book every flight and coordinate arrivals so guests are welcomed together.
