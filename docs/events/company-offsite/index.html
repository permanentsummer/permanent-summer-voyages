Company offsite

Bring the whole team out of the office to work, decide and come back aligned.

The format

The offsite is our most requested format: two to four days with work sessions in the morning, a bonding activity in the afternoon and a dinner people remember. We find the venue with the right meeting room, build the programme with you and book every participant's flights from their own city.

Who is it for?

  • Teams of 15 to 200 people
  • Remote and multi-site companies
  • Fast-growing startups and scale-ups
  • Leadership preparing the year ahead

Sample agenda

  1. Day 1: coordinated arrivals, check-in, welcome dinner
  2. Day 2: strategy in the morning, team activity in the afternoon, evening out
  3. Day 3: breakout workshops, wrap-up, closing dinner
  4. Day 4: free morning or walk, departures

Frequently asked questions

What meeting room for a 60-person offsite?

A plenary room set theatre-style or in pods, plus two or three breakout rooms. We check natural light, AV and wifi before proposing a venue.

Can you facilitate the workshops?

We work with independent facilitators we can suggest. Many teams prefer to run sessions themselves: we then prepare the room and materials.

How much does an offsite cost?

It all depends on the destination, the time of year, the group size and the activities you want. Use our budget calculator for a first estimate, then book a call with our expert and we'll send you tailor-made proposals.
