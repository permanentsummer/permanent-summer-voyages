Team-building retreat

Activities that build real connections, away from screens.

The format

Surfing, hiking, regattas, cooking classes, city treasure hunts: team building works when the activity fits the group. We match the intensity to everyone's level and keep free time in the programme, because that's often where the magic happens.

Group size
10 to 150 people
Length
2 to 3 nights
Book ahead
2 to 4 months

Who is it for?

  • Teams that rarely meet in person
  • New teams after a reorganisation
  • Onboarding new hires
  • Teams who need a breather after a big project

Sample agenda

  1. Day 1: arrivals, icebreaker, barbecue
  2. Day 2: main activity (surf, sailing or hiking), long-table dinner
  3. Day 3: cooking class or city challenge, free evening
  4. Day 4: brunch and departures

Frequently asked questions

What if not everyone is sporty?

We always offer two levels: an active version and a gentler version of the same activity, so nobody is left on the sidelines.

What if it rains?

Every programme has an indoor plan B, booked in advance when needed.

See all activities?

Our team-building library sorts them by energy level, duration and group size.
