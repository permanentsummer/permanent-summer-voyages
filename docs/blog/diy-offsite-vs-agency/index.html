DIY Offsite vs Agency: What You Really Save

Should you plan your company offsite yourself or use an agency? A clear comparison of time, cost, risk and results, with a simple way to decide.

By Christopher Kwo · 21 November 2026 · 3 min read

"We'll just organise it ourselves" is a common decision, and sometimes the right one. But the true cost of a DIY offsite is often hidden. Here is an honest comparison.

What DIY really involves

For a retreat of 20 to 60 people abroad, someone internally will need to:

  • Research destinations and venues, and negotiate contracts.
  • Book flights for every participant, or chase everyone to book their own.
  • Organise transfers, activities, restaurants and meeting equipment.
  • Collect dietary needs, passport details and room preferences.
  • Build the programme and brief facilitators.
  • Handle problems during the trip, often at night.

In our experience this represents 60 to 100 hours of work, usually on top of someone's normal job.

The true cost comparison

DIY Agency
Internal time 60–100 hours 5–10 hours
Venue prices Public rates Often negotiated rates
Flights Booked individually, variable prices Booked centrally, coordinated
Risk on cancellations On you Managed by the agency
Support during the trip Internal organiser Dedicated contact

When DIY makes sense

  • Small groups (under 15) in one city.
  • A venue you already know well.
  • Someone internally with time and experience.
  • A simple programme.

When an agency makes sense

  • Groups over 15 people.
  • A team spread across several cities or countries.
  • A destination you don't know.
  • High stakes: leadership retreats, kickoffs, client events.
  • Nobody internally has 80 spare hours.

Do agencies cost more?

Not necessarily. Agencies often access negotiated venue rates and group fares, and they avoid costly mistakes: late flight bookings, wrong venue contracts, missing extras. The fee is often offset by savings and by the time freed internally.

Always compare the total cost per person, flights included. Our budget calculator helps you build a fair comparison, and our guide to retreat costs in Europe explains each line.

A middle way

Some teams plan the programme internally and hand over the logistics: venue, flights, transfers and activities. That's a format we do often.

Frequently asked questions

Is it cheaper to plan a company offsite yourself?

Not always. Once you count internal time, public rates and individually booked flights, an agency is often similar in cost or cheaper.

How long does it take to plan a company retreat?

Around 60 to 100 hours of work for a group of 20 to 60 people abroad, spread over two to three months.

What does a retreat agency handle?

Venue search and negotiation, programme and activities, transfers, supplier management and support during the trip. We also book every participant's flight.
