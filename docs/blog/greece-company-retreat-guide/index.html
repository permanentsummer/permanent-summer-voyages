Greece for Company Retreats: Athens, Crete, Peloponnese or the Cyclades?

Planning a company retreat in Greece? Compare Athens and the Riviera, Crete, the Peloponnese and the Cyclades, with activities, seasons and budget tips.

By Christopher Kwo · 21 November 2026 · 3 min read

Greece offers something few destinations can: ancient history, mountains and islands, long sunny seasons and a culture built around sharing food. For company retreats, the choice usually comes down to four areas.

Athens and the Athens Riviera

Athens combines world-famous history with a modern food and design scene. Half an hour from the centre, the Athens Riviera has seafront hotels with meeting rooms and sunset views over the Saronic Gulf.

  • Best for: larger groups, kickoffs, mixed city and coast programmes.
  • Ideas: a private Acropolis visit, a street food tour, a sailing trip to Aegina, a cooking class.

Discover Athens for retreats.

Crete

Greece's largest island has everything: gorges, mountains, beaches, villages and a food culture that alone is worth the trip. Long sunny seasons make it ideal from April to late October.

  • Best for: retreats combining nature and work, incentive trips.
  • Ideas: hiking the White Mountains, a village cooking class, olive oil tasting, a jeep safari.

Discover Crete.

The Peloponnese

Ancient sites such as Olympia and Epidaurus, olive groves, quiet coastal towns and high-end resorts. Calmer and less crowded than the islands.

  • Best for: leadership retreats and teams who want privacy.

Discover the Peloponnese.

The Cyclades (Paros, Naxos)

Whitewashed villages, blue doors and sailing between islands. Paros and Naxos are more relaxed and better value than the most famous islands, and easier for groups.

  • Best for: small incentive groups and memorable rewards.
  • Note: access usually means a flight to Athens plus a ferry or short flight, so plan the logistics carefully.

Discover the Cyclades.

When to go

April to June and September to October are ideal. July and August are hot and busy, especially on the islands.

Getting there

Athens and Heraklion (Crete) have direct flights from many European cities in season. For island retreats, group logistics matter even more: arrivals, ferry times and transfers need to line up. That's where booking every participant's flight centrally really helps.

Budget

For three nights including flights, plan roughly €1,200 to €2,300 per person depending on the area and season. Try our budget calculator.

Frequently asked questions

Is Greece good for a corporate retreat?

Yes. It offers sun for much of the year, great food, history and nature, with options from city hotels in Athens to quiet resorts in the Peloponnese.

Which Greek island is best for a team offsite?

Crete is the easiest for groups thanks to its airports and range of venues. Paros and Naxos suit smaller groups looking for an island atmosphere.

When is the best time for a company retreat in Greece?

May, June, September and early October offer warm weather without the peak summer crowds.
