TeamOut Alternatives: How to Choose the Right Retreat Partner

Looking for TeamOut alternatives? A fair comparison of retreat planning options in Europe and the key questions to ask, including who books your team's flights.

By Christopher Kwo · 21 November 2026 · 3 min read

TeamOut is a well-known company retreat platform, used by many international companies to find venues and plan offsites worldwide. It is a solid option. But depending on your team, your destination and how much support you need, other partners may suit you better.

Here is a fair look at what to consider.

What TeamOut is known for

  • A large catalogue of retreat venues around the world.
  • An online experience to browse and compare options.
  • Planners who build proposals for corporate groups.
  • Experience with international companies and large groups.

Why teams look for alternatives

Every team has different priorities. The most common reasons we hear:

  1. Flights for everyone. Distributed teams want the flights of every participant booked and coordinated, not just the venue and programme.
  2. A smaller, more personal service. Some teams prefer a direct relationship with the people planning their trip.
  3. Local knowledge. Teams going to Portugal, Spain, Greece or Poland want planners who know the venues personally.
  4. Budget clarity. One price per person including everything.

Alternatives to consider

Specialist European retreat organisers

Companies such as Surf Office or Lá Fora focus on European retreats with curated venues. See our overview of retreat planners.

Local destination specialists (DMCs)

Great on-the-ground knowledge for one destination, though you may need to coordinate several suppliers yourself.

Doing it in-house

Possible for small, simple retreats. Read DIY vs agency to see the real time cost.

Questions to ask any retreat partner

  • Do you book flights for every participant, from every city?
  • Who is my contact before and during the trip?
  • What's included in the price per person?
  • How do you handle cancellations and changes?
  • Have you visited the venues you propose?

Frequently asked questions

What is TeamOut?

TeamOut is a company retreat platform that helps businesses find venues and plan offsites around the world, with planners to support the process.

What is the best alternative to TeamOut in Europe?

It depends on your needs. If your team is spread across several cities, look for a partner that books every participant's flight, such as Permanent Summer Voyages.

Do retreat platforms include flights?

Not always. Many focus on the venue and programme. Always ask whether flights for every participant are included and coordinated.
