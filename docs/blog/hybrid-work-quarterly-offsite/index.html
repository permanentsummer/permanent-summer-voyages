Hybrid Work and the Rise of the Quarterly Offsite

Why hybrid and remote companies are moving to quarterly offsites, how to plan a lighter meetup format, and how to keep costs and logistics under control.

By Christopher Kwo · 21 November 2026 · 3 min read

Hybrid and remote work have changed what the office is for. When people only meet occasionally, those moments carry more weight. That's why a growing number of companies combine one big annual retreat with smaller, more frequent team offsites, often every quarter.

Why quarterly works

  • Relationships don't last forever on video. Meeting in person every few months keeps trust strong.
  • Planning rhythms are quarterly. A meetup at the start of each quarter aligns the team on priorities.
  • Smaller groups are easier. A team of 8 to 25 can travel and decide faster than the whole company.
  • New hires integrate faster when they don't wait a year to meet colleagues.

The format: lighter and focused

A quarterly offsite doesn't need to be a big trip. A typical format:

  • 2 days, 1 or 2 nights.
  • One city or region with short, direct flights for everyone.
  • One day of focused work on the quarter's priorities.
  • One shared experience: a dinner, a hike, a cooking class.

Rotate the destinations

Keep it fresh by changing cities each quarter, choosing places well connected to your team's hubs:

  • Q1: Seville or Madeira for winter sun.
  • Q2: Lisbon or Mallorca.
  • Q3: Gdańsk or Ericeira.
  • Q4: Kraków or Vienna for festive markets.

Keep costs under control

  • Choose value destinations for the smaller meetups and save the special location for the annual retreat.
  • Book flights centrally and early. With a regular rhythm, you can plan months ahead and keep fares low. We book every participant's flight and coordinate arrivals.
  • Reuse what works. Once you find a great venue, consider returning.

How it fits with the annual retreat

Use the annual retreat for the whole company: big strategy, culture, celebration. Use the quarterly meetups for teams: planning, problem-solving and connection. Read our remote team retreat guide for the bigger event.

Frequently asked questions

What is a quarterly offsite?

A short team meetup held every three months, usually two days long, to align on the next quarter's priorities and strengthen relationships.

How often should hybrid teams meet in person?

Many hybrid teams meet quarterly at team level, plus one larger company-wide retreat per year.

Is a quarterly offsite expensive?

Not necessarily. Short formats in value destinations with direct flights keep costs low, especially when booked early.
