How Much Does a Company Retreat Cost Per Person in Europe?

Real cost ranges for a company retreat in Europe, per person and flights included: venue, meals, activities, transfers, plus the hidden costs most budgets miss.

By Christopher Kwo · 21 November 2026 · 3 min read

"How much will it cost?" is always the first question, and the honest answer is: it depends on five things. Once you understand them, you can build a budget that holds, with no surprises after the invoice arrives.

Below are indicative ranges we see for teams travelling within Europe, for a three-night retreat. Treat them as a starting point, then get a tailored figure from our budget calculator.

The five factors that drive the cost

  1. Where people fly from. A team based in one city is cheaper to move than a team spread across eight countries.
  2. The destination. Kraków or Porto cost less than Mallorca or the Amalfi coast in July.
  3. The season. Shoulder months (March–April, October–November) can save 20 to 35% on venues.
  4. The level of the venue. Three-star hotel, boutique hotel or exclusive-use villa.
  5. What you include. Activities, private dinners, a photographer, a facilitator, gifts.

Indicative ranges per person (3 nights, flights included)

Level What it looks like Indicative range
Smart Good 3–4* hotel, city or coast, shared activities €900 – €1,400
Comfort Boutique hotel or quinta, private dinners, 2 activities €1,400 – €2,200
Premium Exclusive-use villa or 5* hotel, curated programme €2,200 – €3,500+

These ranges assume flights within Europe. Long-haul destinations such as Marrakech remain close to European prices, as flights from most European hubs are short.

Where the money goes

A typical split for a comfort-level retreat:

  • Flights: 20 to 35%. It's the most variable line, and the one most budgets forget. Read how to book group flights.
  • Accommodation: 25 to 35%.
  • Meals and drinks: 15 to 20%.
  • Meeting space and equipment: 5 to 10%. Often included with exclusive venues.
  • Activities: 5 to 15%. See team-building activities.
  • Transfers: 3 to 6%.
  • Contingency: 10%. Always keep it.

The hidden costs most teams miss

  • Late flight bookings. Prices for groups can double in the last six weeks.
  • Tourist taxes. Charged per person per night in Portugal, Spain, Italy and Greece.
  • Meeting room extras. Projectors, flipcharts and coffee breaks can be billed separately.
  • Minimum spends. Many venues require a minimum on food and beverages for exclusive use.
  • Time. An internal organiser spends 60 to 100 hours on a retreat. That cost is real.

How to lower the cost without losing quality

  • Choose the right destination for your team's airports. A direct flight for everyone beats a "cheaper" venue with connections.
  • Go in the shoulder season. Lisbon, Seville and Crete are excellent in October.
  • Book exclusive use. For 15 to 40 people, a whole quinta or villa often costs less than hotel rooms plus meeting rooms.
  • Pick value destinations. Kraków, Budapest and Porto offer great quality for the price.
  • Book flights centrally and early. Group fares and early booking keep the biggest variable under control.

A worked example

25 people from London, Berlin and Madrid, three nights in the Algarve in early October, a boutique hotel, one boat trip, one cooking class, two private dinners. The total typically lands around €1,600 to €1,900 per person, flights included.

The same team in July, booked six weeks before, could easily cost €2,400 or more.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good budget per person for a company retreat?

For three nights in Europe with flights included, €1,200 to €2,000 per person covers a comfortable retreat with a good venue, activities and private dinners.

Are flights usually included in retreat quotes?

Rarely. Most venues and agencies quote the venue and programme only. We include every participant's flight in our proposals so the budget is complete.

When is the cheapest time for a company retreat in Europe?

March to April and October to November usually offer the best value in southern Europe, with good weather and lower venue prices.
