Our story

Two experts, one point of contact

Thirty-five years in travel agencies on one side, international hospitality and company retreats on the other, and the same goal: that every trip goes to plan, or better.

Talk to our expertMessage on WhatsApp

Jennifer Kwo

Founder & travel advisor

My name is Jennifer Kwo. For thirty-five years I've organised travel for international corporations, B2B partners and leisure travellers: flights, hotels, groups, business trips and, above all, the unexpected.

After decades inside large agencies, I chose independence to get back to what matters: knowing every client, answering the phone myself and following every booking until you're home.

“A good trip is often one where you never had to think about the details.”

Christopher Kwo

Co-founder · Company retreats

I'm Christopher. I trained in international hospitality, in some of the most prestigious establishments in Hong Kong and Switzerland, then worked with startups and international companies. Today I'm a company retreat specialist, a traveller and a digital nomad: I know what a team needs from a place to work, switch off and reconnect.

A traveller at heart, I'm always looking for the places that don't make the guidebooks: the table nobody knows, the beach at the end of the path, the hotel that makes the difference. I share those hidden gems with our clients.

Hospitality taught me what makes a stay work from the inside, and I bring that eye to every trip we plan.
