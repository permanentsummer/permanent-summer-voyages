How to plan a company offsite: the complete checklist

Everything we check for every retreat, from six months out to back home, flights included.

  1. 6 months out

    Goals & budget

    • Set the main goal: strategy, bonding, reward or onboarding
    • Estimate headcount and departure cities
    • Set a budget per person, flights included
    • Pick two or three possible windows, avoiding school holidays and busy seasons
  2. 5–4 months out

    Destination & venue

    • Shortlist three destinations by flight time from where the team lives
    • Check the venue: rooms, plenary, breakout rooms, wifi, accessibility
    • Read the cancellation terms and deposit schedule
    • Place an option before signing
  3. 4–3 months out

    Flights

    • Collect each person's departure city, exact passport name and preferences
    • Book group or flexible fares early
    • Agree a common arrival window so everyone starts together
    • Plan for special cases: late arrivals, personal extensions, plus-ones
  4. 3–2 months out

    Programme

    • Balance work, activities and free time
    • Choose activities everyone can do, with a weather plan B
    • Collect dietary and accessibility needs
    • Book a facilitator if needed
  5. 6 weeks out

    Communication

    • Send a participant pack: agenda, flights, what to wear, contacts
    • Confirm the rooming list
    • Check insurance and travel documents
    • Set up a WhatsApp group for the trip
  6. 2 weeks out

    Final checks

    • Confirm final numbers with the venue and suppliers
    • Arrange transfers by arrival wave
    • Test the meeting-room AV
    • Print the agenda
  7. During

    On site

    • One contact reachable at any hour
    • A quick check-in with the venue every morning
    • Handle cancelled or changed flights in real time
  8. After

    Wrap-up

    • Send a feedback survey
    • Share photos and decisions
    • Reconcile invoices
    • Note what to keep for next year
