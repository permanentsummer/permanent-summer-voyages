FAQ: a retreat in Gdańsk & Sopot

Why choose Gdańsk & Sopot for a retreat?

A colourful Hanseatic city and the Baltic beaches.

How do we get there?

Direct flights to Gdańsk from many European cities (about 2 h). We book every participant's flights from their own city and coordinate arrivals.

When is the best time to go?

May to September.

Where can a group stay?

Design hotels on the quay, seafront hotels in Sopot. Above 60 people, we combine venues or book out a whole hotel.

What team activities are there?

Workshop in a former shipyard, Sailing on Gdańsk Bay, Bike ride to Sopot pier, Fish dinner on the quay.

What budget should we plan?

It depends on the number of nights, venue level and departure cities. Our calculator gives a first range, and our 3 proposals detail the exact budget per person, flights included.
