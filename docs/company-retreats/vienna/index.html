FAQ: a retreat in Vienna

Why choose Vienna for a retreat?

Palaces, Viennese cafés and vineyards on the edge of town.

How do we get there?

Direct flights from most European cities (about 1 h 30–2 h 30). We book every participant's flights from their own city and coordinate arrivals.

When is the best time to go?

April to October; December for the Christmas markets.

Where can a group stay?

Historic hotels, design hotels. Above 60 people, we combine venues or book out a whole hotel.

What team activities are there?

Workshop in a palace, Dinner at a heuriger, Cycling along the Danube, Viennese pastry class.

What budget should we plan?

It all depends on the destination, the time of year, the group size and the activities you want. Use our budget calculator for a first estimate, then book a call with our expert and we'll send you tailor-made proposals.
