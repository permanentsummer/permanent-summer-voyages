How it works

Four steps, one contact

  1. A call with our expertTo properly understand your team: size, departure cities, dates, budget, goals. We never send proposals without this call.
  2. Tailored proposalsComplete options: venue, programme, activities, flights and budget per person.
  3. We secure the essentialsDestination, venue, meals, meeting room if needed, and flights. This is when you make the first payment.
  4. We finalise the detailsActivities, special dinners and transfers, in a second stage.
  5. We stay on callOne contact on WhatsApp 7 days a week, until everyone is home.

Frequently asked questions

For how many people?

From 10 people, no upper limit. Above 60, we combine several venues or take over a whole hotel.

How far in advance?

Ideally 3 to 6 months ahead, for the best venues and fares. We can also do it in a few weeks.

How is the price set?

Each proposal details the budget per person: venue, flights, activities and our planning fee, agreed upfront. No hidden costs.

Who invoices what?

We invoice everything. You pay only us, in two stages, and we pay the venue, the flights and every supplier. You keep a clear view of every cost.

What budget should we plan?

It all depends on the destination, the time of year, the group size and the activities you want. Use our budget calculator for a first estimate, then book a call with our expert and we'll send you tailor-made proposals.

Our team flies from different cities, is that OK?

That's our speciality. Each participant flies from their own city, coordinated so everyone lands the same day.

What if something goes wrong on site?

You have one contact on WhatsApp 7 days a week. Cancelled flight, weather, surprises: we handle it.
