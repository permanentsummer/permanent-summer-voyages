FAQ: a retreat in Taghazout

Why choose Taghazout for a retreat?

Surf and winter sun on the Atlantic coast.

How do we get there?

Agadir airport (about 3–4 h from many cities), then a 30-minute drive. We book every participant's flights from their own city and coordinate arrivals.

When is the best time to go?

October to April.

Where can a group stay?

Surf houses, boutique hotels. Above 60 people, we combine venues or book out a whole hotel.

What team activities are there?

Surf lessons, Rooftop yoga, Paradise Valley trip, Sunset tagine.

What budget should we plan?

It depends on the number of nights, venue level and departure cities. Our calculator gives a first range, and our 3 proposals detail the exact budget per person, flights included.
