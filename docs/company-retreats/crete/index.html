FAQ: a retreat in Crete

Why choose Crete for a retreat?

Venetian harbours, dramatic gorges and big seafront resorts.

How do we get there?

Direct flights to Heraklion and Chania from many European cities (April to October). We book every participant's flights from their own city and coordinate arrivals.

When is the best time to go?

May, June, September and October.

Where can a group stay?

Resorts with conference rooms, exclusive-use villas. Above 60 people, we combine venues or book out a whole hotel.

What team activities are there?

Gorge hike, Cretan cooking class on a farm, Jeep safari in the mountains, Dinner in Chania harbour.

What budget should we plan?

It depends on the number of nights, venue level and departure cities. Our calculator gives a first range, and our 3 proposals detail the exact budget per person, flights included.
