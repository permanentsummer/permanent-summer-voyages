FAQ: a retreat in Mauritius

Why choose Mauritius for a retreat?

The ultimate reward trip.

How do we get there?

Direct flights from Paris and London (about 11–12 h), or via Dubai. We book every participant's flights from their own city and coordinate arrivals.

When is the best time to go?

May to December.

Where can a group stay?

Resorts, luxury villas. Above 60 people, we combine venues or book out a whole hotel.

What team activities are there?

Catamaran to Île aux Cerfs, Le Morne hike, Kitesurfing or diving, Creole beach dinner.

What budget should we plan?

It depends on the number of nights, venue level and departure cities. Our calculator gives a first range, and our 3 proposals detail the exact budget per person, flights included.
