Why Thessaloniki & Halkidiki for your team

Thessaloniki is northern Greece's liveliest city: a long waterfront, Byzantine heritage and a renowned food scene. The Halkidiki peninsula, with its resorts and pine-fringed beaches, adds the nature side of the trip.

FAQ: a retreat in Thessaloniki & Halkidiki

How many venues do you have in Thessaloniki & Halkidiki?

Our network has 17 group-friendly hotels, villas and venues in Thessaloniki & Halkidiki, with meeting rooms for up to 2,500 people. We shortlist a few based on your dates, budget and team size.

Why choose Thessaloniki & Halkidiki for a retreat?

A young, food-loving seaside city, with the Halkidiki beaches an hour away.

How do we get there?

Thessaloniki airport, direct flights from many European cities. We book every participant's flights from their own city and coordinate arrivals.

When is the best time to go?

April to June, September to October.

Where can a group stay?

City hotels, Halkidiki resorts. Above 60 people, we combine venues or book out a whole hotel.

What team activities are there?

Food tour of the Modiano market, Boat along Mount Athos, Kayaking in Halkidiki, Byzantine monuments walk.

What budget should we plan?

It all depends on the destination, the time of year, the group size and the activities you want. Use our budget calculator for a first estimate, then book a call with our expert and we'll send you tailor-made proposals.
