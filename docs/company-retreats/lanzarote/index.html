FAQ: a retreat in Lanzarote

How many venues do you have in Lanzarote?

Our network has 6 group-friendly hotels, villas and venues in Lanzarote, with meeting rooms for up to 300 people. We shortlist a few based on your dates, budget and team size.

Why choose Lanzarote for a retreat?

Lunar landscapes, César Manrique's art and sun all year.

How do we get there?

Lanzarote airport, direct flights from most European cities (about 4 h). We book every participant's flights from their own city and coordinate arrivals.

When is the best time to go?

Year-round; best October to April.

Where can a group stay?

Design hotels, resorts, villas. Above 60 people, we combine venues or book out a whole hotel.

What team activities are there?

Timanfaya National Park visit, Cycling volcanic roads, Kayaking around Papagayo, César Manrique art trail.

What budget should we plan?

It all depends on the destination, the time of year, the group size and the activities you want. Use our budget calculator for a first estimate, then book a call with our expert and we'll send you tailor-made proposals.
