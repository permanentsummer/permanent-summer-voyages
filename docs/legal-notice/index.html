Our role

Permanent Summer Lda designs and coordinates retreats, offsites and trips. The client pays Permanent Summer Lda for the whole trip, and Permanent Summer Lda pays the venues, accommodation, activities and other suppliers. Flight tickets are issued by our licensed travel agency partner.

Terms

Our proposals and quotes are free and non-binding. A booking is confirmed only after the client's written agreement and, where applicable, payment of the deposit requested by the supplier. Change and cancellation terms are those of each supplier and of the chosen air fares; they are set out in the proposal. Our planning fees are agreed upfront and invoiced by Permanent Summer Lda.

Personal data

Information sent through this site (forms, WhatsApp, email) is used only to answer your request and organise your trip. It is shared with the relevant suppliers (airlines, hotels, venues) only when needed for a booking, and is never sold. This site uses no advertising cookies; appointment booking is provided by Calendly. You can request access to, correction or deletion of your data at contact@permanentsummervoyages.com.

Complaints

For any complaint, write to us at contact@permanentsummervoyages.com. You can also use the Livro de Reclamações (Portuguese electronic complaints book).
