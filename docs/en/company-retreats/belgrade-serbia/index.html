FAQ: a retreat in Belgrade & Serbia

Why choose Belgrade & Serbia for a retreat?

A capital that comes alive at night, and wild nature a few hours away.

How do we get there?

Direct flights from many European cities (about 2–3 h). We book every participant's flights from their own city and coordinate arrivals.

When is the best time to go?

May to September.

Where can a group stay?

Design hotels in Belgrade, nature lodges. Above 60 people, we combine venues or book out a whole hotel.

What team activities are there?

Rafting on the Drina, Evening on a river barge, Kalemegdan fortress, Traditional dinner with live music.

What budget should we plan?

It depends on the number of nights, venue level and departure cities. Our calculator gives a first range, and our 3 proposals detail the exact budget per person, flights included.
