FAQ: a retreat in Azores

Why choose Azores for a retreat?

Volcanoes, green lakes and hot springs in the middle of the Atlantic.

How do we get there?

About 2 h 30 from Lisbon; some direct flights from Europe. We book every participant's flights from their own city and coordinate arrivals.

When is the best time to go?

May to October.

Where can a group stay?

Nature hotels, country houses. Above 60 people, we combine venues or book out a whole hotel.

What team activities are there?

Whale watching, Hot-spring bathing, Crater-lake hike, Lunch cooked in volcanic ground.

What budget should we plan?

It depends on the number of nights, venue level and departure cities. Our calculator gives a first range, and our 3 proposals detail the exact budget per person, flights included.
