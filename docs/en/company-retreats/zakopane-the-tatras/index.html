FAQ: a retreat in Zakopane & the Tatras

Why choose Zakopane & the Tatras for a retreat?

Wooden chalets, peaks and thermal baths at the foot of the Tatras.

How do we get there?

Kraków airport, then about a 2-hour drive. We book every participant's flights from their own city and coordinate arrivals.

When is the best time to go?

June to September for hiking, December to March for snow.

Where can a group stay?

Exclusive-use wooden chalets, spa hotels. Above 60 people, we combine venues or book out a whole hotel.

What team activities are there?

Hike to Morskie Oko lake, Skiing or snowshoeing, Open-air thermal baths, Highlander dinner with live folk music.

What budget should we plan?

It depends on the number of nights, venue level and departure cities. Our calculator gives a first range, and our 3 proposals detail the exact budget per person, flights included.
