FAQ: a retreat in Florence & Tuscany

Why choose Florence & Tuscany for a retreat?

A villa in the hills, Florence for the day.

How do we get there?

Florence and Pisa airports (about 2 h from many cities). We book every participant's flights from their own city and coordinate arrivals.

When is the best time to go?

April to June, September–October.

Where can a group stay?

Villas, agriturismi, wine estates. Above 60 people, we combine venues or book out a whole hotel.

What team activities are there?

Chianti tasting, Tuscan cooking class, Cycling through the vineyards, Private Florence tour.

What budget should we plan?

It depends on the number of nights, venue level and departure cities. Our calculator gives a first range, and our 3 proposals detail the exact budget per person, flights included.
