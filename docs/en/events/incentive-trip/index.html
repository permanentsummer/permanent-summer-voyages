The format

An incentive trip is a reward, not an offsite in disguise: a beautiful hotel, exclusive experiences and almost no meetings. We handle everything, including premium-cabin flights and plus-ones.

10 to 100 people
Length

Who is it for?

  • Top sales performers
  • President's clubs
  • Partners and distributors
  • Teams after a big win

Sample agenda

  1. Day 1: VIP welcome, gala dinner
  2. Day 2: private boat, free afternoon
  3. Day 3: exclusive experience (tasting, helicopter, chef's table)
  4. Day 4: spa or excursion, closing dinner

Frequently asked questions

Can partners come along?

Yes, it's common for this format. We handle their flights and a tailored programme.

Can we go long-haul?

Yes: Mauritius, Morocco or South Africa are classics. We book the long-haul flights together with everything else.
